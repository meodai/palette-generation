The same 24 steps,
two different wheels

Outer ring — RYB. Itten's wheel. The one from every colour theory class, built out of how paint mixes.

Inner disc — HSL. Straight off the RGB cube. The one your code has.

They agree at red, at twelve o'clock, and drift from there. RYB's green-to-blue span is stretched across twice as much of the HSL wheel — which is why the two rings are furthest apart down the bottom.
