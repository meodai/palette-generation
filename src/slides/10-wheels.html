The same 24 steps,
two different wheels

Outer ring — RYB. Itten's wheel. The one from every color theory class, built out of how paint mixes.

Inner disc — HSL. Straight off the RGB cube. The one your code has.

They agree at red, at twelve o'clock, and drift from there. RYB takes a third of the wheel to get from red to yellow; HSL takes a sixth — so the warm side runs ahead, and the rings are furthest apart between yellow and green.
